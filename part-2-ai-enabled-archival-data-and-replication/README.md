# Part 2 — AI-Enabled Archival Data and Replication

> **Required.** Modules 8–9.

Module 8 builds one CIK-year panel from EDGAR — the 10-K itself, its XBRL financials as originally reported, and the Item 4.02 8-Ks that later declared a year unreliable — benchmarking an LLM extractor against regex on a hand-labeled gold set. Module 9 uses that panel to put a published idea to work — that the topics a 10-K discusses help predict restatements — following the design in Brown, Crowley & Elliott (2020, *JAR*).
